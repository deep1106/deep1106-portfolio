# Deep Bhavsar Portfolio Design Direction

## Experience mode
Experience / recruiter-facing portfolio. The visitor should understand Deep's positioning in one viewport, scan credible work quickly, and reach out without friction.

## Visual direction
A restrained dark editorial-tech system: near-black canvas, indigo accent used for emphasis, high-contrast typography, thin borders, and subtle depth. Avoid decorative gradients, emoji icons, fake metrics, and motion that competes with content.

## UX priorities
1. Clear positioning: AI automation engineer and n8n workflow specialist.
2. Fast scanning: About, Projects, Workflows, Contact remain visible in predictable navigation.
3. Evidence over claims: link directly to GitHub projects and the Shayona workflow repository.
4. Mobile-first navigation with a keyboard-accessible menu.
5. Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve visible focus states.

## Content guardrails
Do not invent experience duration, client satisfaction metrics, testimonials, employers, or project outcomes. Use factual skills, projects, links, and contact details supplied by Deep.

## Tokens
- Canvas: `#030303`
- Surface: `#111114`
- Text: `#f5f5f7`
- Muted text: `#a1a1a6`
- Accent: `#6366f1`
- Radius: 12–24px
- Motion: 150–400ms, with reduced-motion fallback
